Safety comes first — always

Bows and Spoons is a companion, not a medical device. It does not diagnose allergy, and it never replaces your clinician's advice. The app keeps a one-tap emergency plan and a 911 dial on every screen. If your baby has trouble breathing, swelling lips or tongue, goes pale or floppy, or vomits repeatedly — call 911 now. Talk with your pediatrician before you begin.
